Questions

Helvetic Tech is located in La Tour-de-Peilz, in the Lake Geneva region of Switzerland. The campus is situated in the Swiss Riviera, with convenient access to cities such as Lausanne and Geneva, as well as major European destinations.

Helvetic Tech combines Swiss academic standards with a practical, industry-focused approach. Programs are designed to integrate technical knowledge with real-world applications, supported by small class sizes and a personalized learning environment.

Helvetic Tech offers bachelor’s and master’s programs in fields such as artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and blockchain, with a strong focus on emerging technologies.

Dual degrees are programs delivered in partnership between Helvetic Tech and Tiffin University. Upon successful completion, students receive both a Swiss qualification from Helvetic Tech and an American degree from Tiffin University.

Applications are submitted online through the application form. You can start your application and submit any missing documents once they become available, allowing you to secure your place while completing your file.

Admission requirements depend on the level of study. For bachelor’s programs, applicants must hold a recognized secondary school diploma. For master’s programs, applicants must hold a relevant undergraduate degree or equivalent. Applicants may also need to demonstrate English proficiency if previous studies were not conducted in English.

Applicants are typically required to submit a completed application form, academic transcripts and diplomas, identification such as a passport or equivalent, and proof of English proficiency if applicable. Additional documents may be requested depending on the program.

Deferral options may be available depending on the intake and individual circumstances. Applicants should contact the admissions team for specific guidance.

Students from outside the EU/EEA generally require a student visa. The process is handled through the Swiss embassy or consulate in the applicant’s country.

Helvetic Tech provides the necessary documentation and guidance to support students throughout the visa application process.

Yes, health insurance is mandatory for all students in Switzerland. Students must obtain coverage that meets Swiss legal requirements upon arrival.

Students can choose from a range of accommodation options in and around La Tour-de-Peilz and nearby areas such as Vevey. These include shared apartments and private housing, depending on availability and budget.

Helvetic Tech offers a limited number of accommodation options reserved for its students, allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. The institution also provides guidance to help students find suitable housing in the surrounding area.

Accommodation is typically located close to the campus or within short commuting distance, particularly in La Tour-de-Peilz and surrounding areas.

Helvetic Tech offers three intakes per year, in September, January, and April.

After receiving an offer, students must confirm their place by completing the required administrative steps, including submitting documents and paying the admission fee.

Students typically receive their timetable during the registration week before the start of the academic term.

Students are advised to arrive at least one week before the start of the term to complete administrative formalities and settle in.

Geneva Airport is the closest international airport, with direct train connections to La Tour-de-Peilz.

Upon arrival, students receive guidance on registration, residence permits, and orientation to help them transition smoothly into student life.

Programs are delivered in a blended format that combines on-campus learning with online components, offering both structure and flexibility.

Helvetic Tech maintains small class sizes to ensure personalized support, close interaction with faculty, and an effective learning environment.

Students benefit from academic support, mentorship, and career guidance throughout their studies, as well as assistance with administrative and practical aspects of student life.
