Program overview

Bachelor of Science inArtificial Intelligence

The Bachelor of Science in Artificial Intelligence at Helvetic Tech provides a strong foundation in artificial intelligence, combining technical knowledge with practical application. The program is designed to prepare students to understand, develop, and apply AI technologies in real-world environments.

Through a hands-on, project-based approach, students explore key areas such as machine learning, neural networks, and generative AI. The curriculum emphasizes problem-solving, innovation, and collaboration, enabling students to work on real use cases and interact with industry-relevant tools and methodologies.
