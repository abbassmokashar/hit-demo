Program overview

Bachelor of Science inBlockchain

The program provides a strong foundation in blockchain systems, distributed networks, and cryptographic principles, while also addressing the growing role of blockchain across industries.

From early in the program, students work on projects involving blockchain architecture, smart contracts, and decentralized applications, gaining experience that reflects how blockchain is used in real-world environments.
