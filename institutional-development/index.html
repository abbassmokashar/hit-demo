Institutional Development

Institutional Development

Helvetic Tech is a recently established institution designed to respond to the current and future demands of technology-driven industries.

01

A modern approach to development

Helvetic Tech is structured to evolve alongside the industries it serves. Its development focuses on maintaining flexibility in program design and continuously aligning academic content with technological advancements.

02

Academic program development

Programs at Helvetic Tech are built around current and emerging areas of technology, including artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, and blockchain.

03

Educational partnerships

Helvetic Tech operates in partnership with Tiffin University, offering a dual-degree structure that combines a Swiss qualification with a US-accredited degree.

04

Quality and continuous improvement

Helvetic Tech implements structured processes to monitor and improve its academic and operational activities.

05

Faculty and learning environment

Faculty at Helvetic Tech are active professionals, ensuring that teaching remains connected to current industry practices.

06

Technology integration

Technology is central to both the curriculum and the overall learning experience.

07

Student environment and support

Helvetic Tech provides a structured and supportive environment for students, with services designed to facilitate both academic progression and adaptation to life in Switzerland.
